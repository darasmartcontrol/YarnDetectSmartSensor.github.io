About Dara Smart Control

Dara Smart Control develops industrial embedded systems, intelligent sensing platforms, and automation solutions integrating electronics, firmware, control systems, and mechanical engineering.

The company provides complete product development from concept and electronic design to embedded firmware, mechanical integration, prototype development, and industrial validation.

Engineering Capabilities

Embedded Systems

STM32-based firmware development, real-time embedded programming, ADC/DMA acquisition, sensor interfaces, communication protocols, and motor control.

Electronics & PCB Design

Industrial PCB design, mixed-signal circuits, power electronics, communication interfaces, and hardware validation.

Industrial Automation

Machine control systems, PID controllers, process automation, HMI integration, and industrial monitoring.

Mechanical Development

Industrial product design, sensor housings, assembly design, 3D CAD modeling, and prototype development.
